* Scope of this checklist

The checklist follows the original paper's concrete typed constructions.
The explicit cache/effect inference and unspecified automation proposals remain
research boundaries. Whole-language equivalence and verification of Lean's
C++ primitives, runtime or compiler are outside this task and are not paper
implementation gaps. Existing platform controls are supporting project evidence.
The ~whole_paper_implemented~ flag retains its stronger legacy meaning and stays
false; it does not mark the concrete construction list above as incomplete.
