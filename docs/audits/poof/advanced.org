* Appendix A: executable C3 and checks

[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3.lean][C3.linearize]] implements the ordinary finite paper graph policy separately
from the main C4 object policy. [[file:../../../Tests/C3.lean][C3]] asserts all four source orders,
five refusal cases, both helper checks and 640 comparisons across 160 ordered
parent DAGs against an independent recursive merge oracle.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3Semantics.lean][C3Semantics]] proves the paper's leftmost eligible selection and normalized
deletion step agree with the certified merger for every candidate list. Every
certified output has a paper-style source trace; any source trace over the
same candidates has that output. The [[file:../../contracts/c3-merge.org][reusable C3 guide]] shows how callers retain
the certificate to prove parent-order preservation, coverage and no repeats.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3.lean][C3]] proves one-root batch/scalar equality for every graph and error outcome.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3GraphTrace.lean][C3GraphTrace]] gives every successful uncached result a recursive paper-style
graph derivation. The derivation determines a unique order even when two
successful runs use different fuel. It also erases the fuel index into a
unique paper relation. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3GraphSemantics.lean][C3GraphSemantics]] proves empty/insert preservation and cache-hit
agreement for memo entries carrying that relation, and adds an optional
receipt that transfers derivations to cached results on admitted inputs.
The production visit now preserves the invariant on success, so ordinary
scalar and batch success directly implies a paper derivation. The uncached
batch validates once, including empty roots. Both batch APIs have identical
outcomes for empty roots and for every graph-validation failure; both
one-root batch/scalar laws include exact error results.
Two further kernel theorems cover nonempty batches whose first root is absent
or lists itself as its first parent, including arbitrary later roots.
[[file:../../contracts/c3-graph.org][The graph guide]] distinguishes this zero-replay proof from admission's
recomputation cost. The batch lifting theorem reduces complete outcome
equivalence to one-root coherence on every derivation-backed cache. That
premise is proved for validated graphs without parent edges, yielding exact
outcomes for arbitrary roots, repeated hits and later missing roots. It is
also proved for validated graphs with any finite number of distinct leaf parents per node, including shared
parents and opposite declarations across roots. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3DAG.lean][C3DAG]] now proves complete cached/reference outcomes for finite DAGs of
arbitrary depth under bounded decreasing ranks, including missing parents and
inconsistent merges. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Object/C3.lean][Object.C3]] connects the certified order to actual slot
composition, and [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Object/Recursive.lean][Object.Recursive]] supplies safe dynamic recursive reads.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3CacheCoherence.lean][C3CacheCoherence]] extends complete scalar/batch outcome equality to every
finite graph and root list, including cycles and validation failures. Successful
derivations cannot contain a nonempty self walk. Actual active-path invariants
and the finite declaration count justify each sound cache hit at the production
budget. [[file:../../../Tests/C3CacheCoherence.lean][Coherence controls]] compare complete results to an independent scalar
oracle, including concrete cycle names, first errors and warm ancestor reuse.
Two successful executable traversals now provably agree, so admission omits a
runtime order-comparison branch.

The three extra checks are asserted in [[file:../../../Tests/PrototypeChecks.lean][PrototypeChecks]], with explicit
typed JSON adaptation for mixed/improper-list rejection. [[file:checks-v1.json][The check map]] records
all 24 original expressions. The three c3-extra-definitions helpers are implemented by Dictionary.fromList,
toList and merge, with 594 scalar merge controls. Whole-policy C3/C4 equivalence
remains a separate claim.

* Appendices C, D and E

Appendix C (2155–2255) supplies explanatory type notation. Lean's dependent
types replace that notation; no source row/subtyping calculus translation
theorem is supplied. This is a formal correspondence question, rather than
a missing literal notation parser.

Appendix D (2256–2389) explains multiple ways to tie fixed points in eager and
lazy languages. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/FixedPoint.lean][sharedFix]] uses an unsafe private reference cell and one shared
thunk. [[file:../../../Tests/DebugObject.lean][DebugObject]] and bounded diagnostics cover selected recursion failures.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/CheckedFunction.lean][rebuildingFix]] supplies the source rebuild-per-call contrast;
[[file:../../../Tests/FixedPoints.lean][FixedPoints]] checks 65 recursive inputs and one versus 32 constructions.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/FiniteFix.lean][FiniteFix]] now defines pure finite unfoldings of ~fix~ and the one-step
~mix~ equation. Adjacent equality constructs a proof-bearing fixed point and
implies stability at every later depth. [[file:../../../Tests/FiniteFix.lean][FiniteFix]] proves a two-step Boolean fixed point
over its entire input domain, proves the triangular result for every natural
input at sufficient depth, and compares 65 observations with the shared thunk.
Internal-define/letrec/lazy routes share the Lean knot; direct untyped self-application
is a language choice. There is no universal termination theorem or all-variants operational
equivalence. The paper permits divergence; such a theorem is not required to
implement unrestricted executable recursion. Safe finite dependency schedules
have a stronger, separate contract and do not certify every unsafe knot.

[[file:../../contracts/shared-thunks.org][The shared-thunk guide]] now specifies the declared Interaction compiler and
its safe admission boundary. The actual native knot produces demand traces;
completed trace executions and safe evaluations are equivalent, and the safe
checker compares actual reported values/errors against the exact current
program. The sequential private-cell model proves installation/current-body
and one-constructor invariants. Checked C3 field/method reads transfer to the
existing source graph laws. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/SharedCellRefinement.lean][SharedCellRefinement]] now interprets the single
constructor program that ~sharedFix~ itself executes. Its sequential primitive
rules refine every step/trace to the protocol and prove one result build and
at most one forwarding Ref read. Observed IO operations and reported results
are safely checked together, including publication ordering and pointer
readbacks. The source pin specifies the rules; verification of C++/reference
counting/compiler/concurrent memory and arbitrary unchecked callbacks remains
outside this refinement. A valid data log does not authenticate IO origins.

Appendix E (2390–2434) compares Scheme source sizes. The character/cons-cell
figures are illustrative and do not impose Lean line-count requirements.

* Future Work and related literature

Related Work (1560–1800) describes other systems and research; reproducing each
system is not a feature requirement. Future Work (1801–1830) proposes fuller
generalized prototypes, meta-level type/logical constraints and staging/cache
research. Those proposals must not be counted as completed formal results.
Conversely, their open status alone does not invalidate the existing core
executable construction. The audit preserves both distinctions.

* Composed metadata and multi-argument extension policies

[[file:../../contracts/closed-metadata.org][Automatic metadata closure]] connects the checked source C3 program to unique
closed context-indexed factories using supported finite dependency/locality
evidence. [[file:../../contracts/dispatch-extensions.org][Protected generic extensions]] implements the explicit negative-cache

Both have universal contracts and independent complex workloads; neither infers
arbitrary host closure properties or hidden cross-library assumptions.

* Alternative combinations and coupled publication

The [[file:../../contracts/alternative-combinations.org][alternative-combination contracts]] add eight reusable typed policies and universal ordered reference/failure-state proofs for source 2060–2083. Persistent append is not destructive nconc; empty extrema and last-result groups explicitly return none.

This is an explicit immutable application policy over existing constructions, rather than a new source-required generalized prototype protocol.

* Research extension: initial unified protocol

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-prototypes.org][Generalized protocols]] and its two complex clients advance the explicit future-work proposal at 1807–1827 and 2084–2154. Existing delayed inheritance, nested lenses, C3, standard/simple method groups and dictionary bridges now share one raw/cooked/fragment protocol. Assembly, frame, self-view, finite representation and witnessed-closure laws are proved. This remains a typed research extension: full reflective MOPs, arbitrary host effects, automatic type inference and staging are not claimed. The future-work review unit remains ~future_proposal~ and the original construction closure is unchanged.

The [[file:../../contracts/generalized-components.org][component extension]] advances the same future proposal by mounting independently closed protocols through lawful nested focuses. Exact partition, finite projection, closure joining/projecting, independent regrouping and retained-left replacement laws reuse existing Focus and representation bridges. Regrouping changes construction layout only; it neither reorders public effects nor certifies an arbitrary new C3 topology.

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-dependencies.org][Directional dependency protocols]] advance the same research unit. Shared storage partition is reused independently of cooking compatibility. Downstream closure is indexed by a selected upstream view; same-view reuse is proof-bearing and changed views require fresh evidence. The finite recurrence explicitly uses the previous approximation. This is a triangular protocol, not automatic arbitrary dependency/effect/termination inference.

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-chain.org][Multi-level dependency chains]] continue the same future proposal: finite heterogeneous publications carry exact selected-view indices, existing refresh controls suffix reuse, and nested product mounting provides a whole fixed point. First-refusal laws and independent four-level controls qualify this extension. The original concrete closure map remains unchanged.

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-fork.org][Shared-upstream fork/join composition]] advances the same future proposal: independent branches retain exact local closure evidence, joins inspect the complete refreshed pair, and nested existing close proofs certify a whole diamond with one shared root. Independent controls retain first-refusal and full-ledger observations; ordered metadata has a kernel negative swapping theorem.

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-context.org][Shared context across multiple fork/join levels]] continues this future proposal. Explicit context projections and contextual join keys prevent a retained intermediate join or retained branches from hiding changed shared dependencies. A reusable seven-component workflow carries whole closure and retained-method proofs; full refusal-mask and finite-timing controls qualify the concrete composition.

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-revision.org][Scoped policy publication]] continues the same future proposal: actual policy values join dependency keys, compatible-self recooking retains raw closure, and a ten-component client preserves full errors while distinguishing changed public methods. Numerical metadata equality alone does not license retaining a changed-policy interface.

[[file:../../contracts/generalized-mop.org][Automatic generalized construction and checked MOP]] advances the same future proposal with finite declaration closure, inferred pure footprints and transitive invalidation, actual typed policy inspection and complete source/topology/base/layout/dispatch migration. Existing computation congruence, dependency scheduling, ranked closure, lens construction and multimethod reference semantics supply the proofs. Independent controls cover 96 configurations, 4608 call outcomes, 96 accepted migrations, 1152 refusals and 468 finite node observations. Opaque graph callbacks and cycles refuse construction; typed policy hooks remain explicit and candidates are fully rebuilt. This does not claim unrestricted reflection, arbitrary type/effect inference or external-effect rollback.

[[file:../../contracts/inherited-mop.org][Inherited generalized fragments]] connect the future proposal’s delayed-super wrapper to automatic graph closure and protected MOP publication. Existing computation combinators elaborate replacement, strict modifiers and conditional inheritance; compilation is proved equal to existing delayed lens composition. Source C3 derivations bind exact class-local fragment order to actual closure/dispatch, with complete source/cache preservation on refusal. Independent controls cover 96 settings, 4608 call outcomes, 96 accepted migrations and 576 matrix refusals, plus exact C3 failures and dead-parent pruning. The same contract records remaining staged execution reuse, heterogeneous generic families, declarative metadata and carrier-changing integration boundaries. The future-work status and original concrete closure remain unchanged.

[[file:../../contracts/staged-publication.org][Materialized construction and generic families]] advance the staged-computation/cache-invalidation future proposal. Actual cached results, including failures and absence, replace reevaluation for certified clean nodes; existing ranked uniqueness proves complete reference equality. Typed edit frames infer unchanged resolvers, and refusing retained builders discriminate actual skipping. Existing dependent DispatchTable entries carry per-generic origin, negative and cache-soundness receipts; exact-candidate approval publishes the entire heterogeneous family or the exact warm old version. Controls cover 24 settings, 240 accepted migrations, 288 refusals, 7920 calls and 1320 rebuilt/2304 retained nodes across accepted revisions. Arbitrary source callback equality, automatic type/effect inference, lossy/carrier-changing cooking and cyclic equation closure retain their earlier boundaries.

[[file:../../contracts/declarative-metadata.org][Declarative method, type and logic metadata]] advances generalized-MOP future work with executable method declarations, typed refinement contracts, decidable logical formulas and exact-candidate service publication. Universal origin, invocation, rejection and construction-retention proofs accompany independent truth-table, arithmetic and warm-cache controls.
