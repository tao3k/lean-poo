* Acceptance boundary

The paper gives executable Scheme/Scribble constructions and mathematical
explanations, rather than a machine-checked small-step specification. A claim
that the Lean library implements the whole paper needs an explicit relation
between source observations and Lean observations, beyond matching named
features or counting translated files. The current ~whole_paper_implemented~
flag stays false until the relevant obligations below have actual evidence.
The paper's explicit future proposals are separately classified; they are
not silently promoted into specified executable behavior.

* First mechanized step

[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/FiniteFix.lean][FiniteFix]] supplies total finite unfoldings of the minimal nucleus.
For any prototype, equality of adjacent unfoldings yields a kernel-checked
fixed-point certificate and equality at every later finite depth.
[[file:../../../Tests/PaperFiniteFix.lean][PaperFiniteFix]] proves a complete two-value-domain fixed point and a
triangular result for every natural input at sufficient depth. Its comparison
with the existing shared thunk covers 65 concrete inputs. This is a bounded
observation, not a proof about arbitrary source programs or divergence.

[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/RecordDispatch.lean][RecordDispatch]] makes the paper's error-reporting function-record
boundary explicit. [[file:../../../Tests/PaperRecordDispatch.lean][PaperRecordDispatch]] proves that the paper-style x/y fragment and
typed record have identical checked observations for every String key at
every finite unfolding depth. The source fragment's final-self reads keep
their error propagation; its reachable x/y reads are proved present. At
depth two, the source dispatcher has a fixed-point certificate and the typed
record's checked observations remain stable. This does not extend the
correspondence to arbitrary Scheme values, effects or unrestricted recursion.

[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3Semantics.lean][C3Semantics]] proves the Appendix A merge selector and normalized step
agree with the certified Lean merger for every candidate list. It constructs
a paper-style trace from each certificate and proves any source trace yields
that same order, with inherited orders preserved, exact name coverage and no
duplicates. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3.lean][C3]] also proves that one-root batched and scalar traversal are equal
for every graph and error outcome. [[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3GraphTrace.lean][C3GraphTrace]] constructs a recursive
source-style derivation for every successful cache-free run: each node has a
graph lookup, matching parent derivations, the paper's merge trace and a
certified order trace. The actual direct-parent row is proved a sublist of the
duplicate-free merged tail.
The graph relation determines a unique result for the same graph and root,
independent of successful recursion fuel; positional root batches are unique
too. The fuel-independent relation below makes that uniqueness usable across
memoized visits at different recursion depths.
The fuel-independent ~GraphDerivation~ retains lookup, positional parent and
paper merge premises across memo reuse. ~CacheDerivations.empty~ and ~insert~
preserve proven entries; ~agrees~ compares any proven hit with an independent
paper derivation. ~visit_sound~ proves the actual production parent loop
preserves that invariant on success. ~linearizeMany_derivation~ gives every
successful cached batch a paper derivation without running the reference.
When cached and uncached executions both succeed, uniqueness proves their
outputs equal; admission no longer compares result lists at runtime.
The uncached batch now validates once before visiting roots, including an
empty root list. The previous empty-root behavior accepted malformed graphs;
~linearizeMany_nil_eq_uncached~ and
~linearizeMany_eq_uncached_validation_error~ prove exact complete-result
equality for arbitrary empty batches and all graph-validation failures.
~linearizeUncachedMany_singleton~ proves its scalar/batch result and error
identity on every graph.
~linearizeMany_eq_uncached_missing_first~ and
~linearizeMany_eq_uncached_self_parent_first~ additionally prove complete
result equality for two nonempty error paths, with any remaining requested
roots. Both first validate the graph; when validation succeeds, the first
traversal fails with the same ~unknownNode~ or ~cycle~ value before cache reuse.
~linearizeMany_eq_uncached_of_first_scalar_error~ generalizes the batch-tail
step: any matching error from the two scalar calls remains the complete
result after adding arbitrary later roots. It does not prove that the two
scalar calls always fail or succeed together.
[[file:../../../LeanPoo/Prototype/C3GraphSemantics.lean][C3GraphSemantics]] also offers optional admission when callers need an exact
proof that cached and uncached executable results coincide on supplied graph
and roots. That check may recompute shared ancestry exponentially and is
kept out of the ordinary path. General valid-graph, nonempty-root
cached/uncached outcome equivalence and graph-to-composition simulation remain
open; 640 finite graph comparisons remain observation evidence.

The local toolchain does not currently contain Racket. Installing or running
that separate source evaluator is a future verification step; CI continues to
use the pinned source identity and Lean-owned checks without making the
reference checkout a build dependency.
