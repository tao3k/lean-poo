#+TITLE: Preparing CLOS dispatch at publication

* Research basis

Fare describes staging as guarding a precomputed result before reuse, and lists
precomputation of effective methods under Efficient Dispatch. The CLOS design
here stages the pure program of a method combination. A public
=ClosEffectiveMethod= holds the live arguments and has its own identity.
Construction, invocation and observers therefore remain call-time operations.
See [[https://github.com/metareflection/poof/blob/59cecf050c8f2fab480175278c439410f3151cd6/ltuo_10_efficient_object_implementation.scrbl#L666-L673][staging]]
and [[https://github.com/metareflection/poof/blob/59cecf050c8f2fab480175278c439410f3151cd6/ltuo_10_efficient_object_implementation.scrbl#L2199-L2214][Efficient Dispatch]].

* Algorithm

- Reuse the [[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/Multimethod.lean][C4 sparse candidate index]].
  Class and generic generations determine eligible pure input. For an
  unconditional shape, the existing
  [[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/PreparedMultimethod.lean][prepared multimethod proof]]
  permits precomputing the ordered selection. When =eql= or another live guard
  participates, it still selects against the actual arguments.
- Publish a recipe for each proved pure phase over its declared support.
  A recipe can encode arrangement of method groups and the standard
  continuation structure. Slot observations, observer callbacks, hook
  invocation, read issuance, argument carriers and write guards stay live.
- Check the owner identity and its current epoch at the phase's original effect boundary. A hit
  consumes the recipe and skips pure preparation. A stale entry resumes the
  cold calculation at the same phase with the effects already performed.
- After a class, generic, method or dependency edit, advance epochs of owners
  in the dependent closure on the conservative union of old and admitted new
  edges. A change to stage identity or dependency roster dirties its owner.
  Publish graph metadata and epochs as one snapshot using the existing
  component publication path. Retained owners preserve their epochs.

* Graph and proof obligations

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07197v3#S4][Tarjan and Zwick's Algorithm T]]
supplies a cold SCC algorithm with linear graph work. Existing
[[file:../../LeanPoo/Functional/LocalSccRegion.lean][local SCC repair]]
proves equivalence of component replacement and cold construction.
Propagation to dependent owners also follows acyclic paths outside the
SCC repair region. Keep the existing POO reverse index and SCC accelerator.

[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/PublishedDispatch.lean][PublishedDispatch]]
proves stable support from the affected-closure rule, zero preparation
calls on a current hit, and equality to cold computation of the entire
event trace, outcome and world. The model assumes dependency registration
is complete and epoch reads see the corresponding published snapshot.
Each observed action is executed once with live state. The finite control
includes a callback that changes a later phase's input and forces a
rejection without repeating the earlier event.

* Scheme boundary

The model counts pure preparation calls. It leaves the real number of
stages, dependency enumeration costs, and the cost of observable reads to
the Scheme refinement. The 570-event contract makes the ordered event
sequence a lower bound on deliveries. Extract the pure stage boundaries
from dispatch and the existing issued-read path before applying this
design. Do not replace a repeated observed check with a receipt from
before a callback.
