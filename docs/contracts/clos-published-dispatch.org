#+TITLE: Preparing CLOS dispatch at publication

* Research basis

Fare describes staging as guarding a precomputed result before reuse, and lists
precomputation of effective methods under Efficient Dispatch. The CLOS design
here stages the pure program of a method combination. A public
=ClosEffectiveMethod= holds the live arguments and has its own identity.
Construction, invocation and observers therefore remain call-time operations.
See [[https://github.com/metareflection/poof/blob/59cecf050c8f2fab480175278c439410f3151cd6/ltuo_10_efficient_object_implementation.scrbl#L666-L673][staging]]
and [[https://github.com/metareflection/poof/blob/59cecf050c8f2fab480175278c439410f3151cd6/ltuo_10_efficient_object_implementation.scrbl#L2199-L2214][Efficient Dispatch]].

* Algorithm

- Reuse the [[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/Multimethod.lean][C4 sparse candidate index]].
  Class and generic generations determine eligible pure input. For an
  unconditional shape, the existing
  [[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/PreparedMultimethod.lean][prepared multimethod proof]]
  permits precomputing the ordered selection. When =eql= or another live guard
  participates, it still selects against the actual arguments.
- Publish a recipe for each proved pure phase over its declared support.
  A recipe can encode arrangement of method groups and the standard
  continuation structure. Slot observations, observer callbacks, hook
  invocation, read issuance, argument carriers and write guards stay live.
- Check the owner identity and its current epoch at the phase's original effect boundary. A hit
  consumes the recipe and skips pure preparation. A stale entry resumes the
  cold calculation at the same phase with the effects already performed.
- After a class, generic, method or dependency edit, advance epochs of owners
  in the dependent closure on the conservative union of old and admitted new
  edges. A change to stage identity or dependency roster dirties its owner.
  Publish graph metadata and epochs as one snapshot using the existing
  component publication path. Retained owners preserve their epochs.

* Graph and proof obligations

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2201.07197v3#S4][Tarjan and Zwick's Algorithm T]]
supplies a cold SCC algorithm with linear graph work. Existing
[[file:../../LeanPoo/Functional/LocalSccRegion.lean][local SCC repair]]
proves equivalence of component replacement and cold construction.
Propagation to dependent owners also follows acyclic paths outside the
SCC repair region. Keep the existing POO reverse index and SCC accelerator.

[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/PublishedDispatch.lean][PublishedDispatch]]
proves stable support from the affected-closure rule, zero preparation
calls on a current hit, and equality to cold computation of the entire
event trace, outcome and world. The model assumes dependency registration
is complete and epoch reads see the corresponding published snapshot.
Each observed action is executed once with live state. The finite control
includes a callback that changes a later phase's input and forces a
rejection without repeating the earlier event.

* Scheme boundary

The model counts pure preparation calls. It leaves the real number of
stages, dependency enumeration costs, and the cost of observable reads to
the Scheme refinement. The 570-event contract makes the ordered event
sequence a lower bound on deliveries. Extract the pure stage boundaries
from dispatch and the existing issued-read path before applying this
design. Do not replace a repeated observed check with a receipt from
before a callback.

* Refinement by dispatch stage

The reference is Gerbil-POO =d7d438e= and the local =a3add7b=
effective-route variant. The latter has the same distance calculation
and standard combination order.

| Scheme boundary | What can be prepared | What runs at the original boundary |
|-
| =method-distances=, =sort-ranked= | Shape ranks, argument-precedence projection and the stable candidate order | Read each required specializer. Match =eql= against this call's argument. Evaluate guard and rejection conditions. |
| =protocol-ranked-methods= | C4 candidate lookup for an admitted shape | Author filtering, reordering and result validation. A reordered or otherwise open result uses the returned order directly. |
| =clos-methods-with-qualifier= | Normalize admitted declarations once and stably partition the ordered candidates | Deliver the four ordered qualifier read passes where observable. Only a valid phase plan may consume the resulting selection. |
| =select-effective-method=, =execute-standard-effective-method= | Standard group layout and least-specific-first after order | Keyword checks, the no-applicable decision, current arguments and carriers, body reads, method effects and the early no-primary refusal. |
| =call-next-method= and lifecycle write guards | The owner's pure descriptor and plan | Renew applicability for replacement arguments. Authenticate each class and slot check at its own boundary, including after a callback. |

[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/CandidateShape.lean][CandidateShape]]
models the two C4 position domains used in Scheme and the zero rank for
=eql=. Class and prototype targets are kept distinct even when their ranks
tie. The stable filtering theorem in
[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/CandidateOrder.lean][CandidateOrder]]
proves that a pure selection mask gives the same order before or after
sorting, including tied methods. Its sparse lookup corollary uses the
existing prototype-trie proof. A Scheme refinement must account for
both position domains and the original declaration order when admitting
CLOS index entries. The admitted argument-precedence permutation supplies
in-bounds distance indices.

[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/DispatchPreparation.lean][DispatchPreparation]]
composes the candidate proof with one-pass qualifier grouping and
standard recipe selection. A live order-preserving mask may remove an
=eql= candidate without repeating sort, normalization, partition or
after-list reversal. A live hook that can reorder is outside that
equivalence. The grouping theorem takes the admitted qualifier values.
If an observable qualifier read disagrees, prepare the active stage
from the live values and keep the observations it already delivered.
The published plan keeps the most-specific-first reflective
groups and the execution order separately. It contains no current
invocation, prototype validation result, or call outcome.

An effect boundary ends the scope of any plan guard that precedes it.
[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/ObservationResume.lean][ObservationResume]]
proves that completion of one stage hands its world and exact read prefix
to the next stage, and that a post-callback authentication failure
keeps all earlier observations. On a stale hit the new stage is
prepared in that world. Observed specializer reads are sequenced
before combining their truth values, so an earlier =eql= miss cannot
short-circuit the remaining read actions. Preserve the same emitted
records, order and refusal point, with the 570-event fixture as a
conformance oracle.

* Exact invalidation

[[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/DispatchInvalidation.lean][DispatchInvalidation]]
uses the existing old-inverse rewrite theorem and the SCC region
theorem. The affected set corresponds to the reverse cursor walk in
=generalized-graph.ss= (=affected-work=). Its epoch-change set equals the old indexed dependent closure
when edited class or method inputs and every changed dependency row
are seeded. The old/new edge union is for the SCC repair envelope.
Consumers on acyclic paths still invalidate, and all owners of an
affected SCC agree on reachability. These theorems establish the
logical sets. Enumeration and mutation costs belong to the existing
Scheme index implementation and its eventual refinement.

A materialized Class precedence assignment excludes native
=direct-subclasses= writes from its dispatch dependency. The Class
receiver itself still invalidates so ordinary read evidence follows the
new roster. A Class generation change or a changed specializer follows
the whole reverse row. Reaching a scoped receiver through another
dependency widens the visit to its whole row, with one revision advance.
See [[file:../../LeanPoo/Object/ClosAdmission/ClassDispatchDependencies.lean][Class dispatch dependencies]].
