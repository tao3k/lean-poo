#+TITLE: Generic lens fragments and non-record declarations

* Racket, Lean and Gerbil construction

Appendix B's ~$lens-gen~ packages getter/setter/wrapper/method and keeps raw
construction separate from cooked self. This future-work extension uses that
existing generalized compiler with ordinary extensible fragment descriptors and
a declaration macro. It has no closed enumeration of supported raw carriers.

~Generalized.Fragment~ existentially packages each descriptor's Focus and Method
types. ~compile_eq~ proves exact lens construction; ~compile_frame~ and
~fragments_frame~ prove preservation of an explicit frame condition for the actual
fragment list. No arbitrary lens law is silently assumed or discovered.

* Shared declarations

The existing ~DeclarativeMetadata.expandForms~ now abstracts its entry type.
Record and generalized consumers reuse the same ordered Reader expansion.
~elaborateFragments_source~ binds complete admitted descriptors to expansion and
finalization at the SAME current context. Native ~.prototype~ and ~.declare~ share
~declaration-forms~, including static names and computed splicing.

Native preparation admits labels/descriptors, captures versions and compiles
without running wrappers or method bodies. Observation/cooking use existing
finite self unfolding under supported-source guards. External callback metadata,
including mutable components captured by composed lenses, needs explicit sources.
Pure locally total callbacks and immutable inputs are correspondence premises.

* Examples and scope

[[file:../../Examples/DeclaredPrototypes.lean][Declared prototype examples]] prove heterogeneous nested-product construction,
existing standard methods, seed-independent triangular charge after three stages
and a function carrier using the identical protocol. Native counterparts execute
nested vectors with persistent setters, audit preservation, callable cooking,
source revisions, laziness and nonlocal multi-output expansion.

Finite observations and the specific triangular observation law do not establish
an arbitrary raw fixed point, function-identity equality, universal custom lens
correctness, callback termination or Scheme compiler/runtime correctness.
No performance claim or new benchmark data accompanies this functional tranche.
