#+TITLE: Joint publication of closed factories and generic extensions

[[file:../reusable-contracts.org][Reusable APIs]] · [[file:closed-metadata.org][Factory closure]] · [[file:dispatch-extensions.org][Protected extensions]]

* One immutable version

~Functional.FactoryDispatchPublication.Snapshot~ holds a checked metadata
construction, its generic function, the exact generic's negative certificate
and an ~Approved construction generic~ payload. A payload may retain selected
factories, dependent consumer bounds and actual dispatch receipts. It is indexed
by both components; evidence from the old factories cannot simply be inserted
into an unrelated new version.

~stage~ runs a supplied factory-construction thunk, the existing extension
~guard~, then supplied joint admission. Errors retain their phase and payload;
dispatch conflicts and admission refusal retain the actual candidate evidence.
Metadata errors precede dispatch errors, and dispatch errors precede admission.
The construction thunk and admission are explicit typed callbacks; arbitrary
host closures and hidden cross-library assumptions are not inferred.

An accepted ~Revision~ carries exact equations for reconstruction, the complete
dispatch patch, and joint admission of its snapshot. ~Snapshot.closed~ exposes
closure of the actual captured factory program. ~Revision.cacheSound~ transfers
the existing sound-cache invariant through the completed dispatch edits.

~publish~ returns its complete stage result and one snapshot.
~publish_accepted~ selects the accepted snapshot; ~publish_refused~ retains the
entire old version, including its old factories, approvals and warm cache, on
every error. No intermediate version is published. This is an explicit pure
application publication policy; it is not concurrent commit, external IO
rollback, or a new generalized meta-object protocol.

* A coupled pricing upgrade

[[file:../../Examples/JointPublication.lean][JointPublication]] changes the parent order of a shared C3 diamond whose
doubling and extra-fee wrappers do not commute. It upgrades corresponding Card
pricing fragments, protects structural Bank absences, and admits a consumer
only when actual stateful dispatch agrees with its selected total factory and
fits the selected capacity at budget 5. The stored approval certifies that
request; it does not infer agreement for every budget. Refused mismatch,
inherited Bank contribution and actual missing-parent construction all retain
the old warm version; the old price remains 19 and the accepted version is 18.

[[file:../../Tests/FactoryDispatchPublication.lean][FactoryDispatchPublication]] compares 512 complete publication outcomes
against an independent arithmetic/phase oracle. It varies old/new rates,
metadata refusal, bad arity, Bank conflicts, canceled extensions, joint mismatch,
capacity refusal and cold/warm old caches, including simultaneous errors.

This combines the existing concrete source constructions at 1254–1314
(metadata), 1980–2059 (generic extension) and 2060–2083 (combination), with
explicit consumer admission. The paper does not prescribe this application's
atomic publication or error-priority policy.
