#+TITLE: Nested focused updates and admitted method fragments

* Focus a concrete part of a production value

[[file:../../LeanPoo/Functional/FocusedUpdate.lean][FocusedUpdate]] supplies ~Focus~: an ordinary getter and setter with explicit
get-after-set, set-current and last-set laws. ~compose~ retains those laws for
nested parts. ~modify~ accumulates changes on the current value;
~compose_modify~ identifies a nested update with successive focusing and
~modify_modify~ preserves the exact order of updates on one part.
~toMonoLens~ connects to the existing skew/monomorphic lens implementation.
~Factory.focus~ lifts the update through a context-indexed factory, including
dependent part/whole families; ~focus_get~ identifies the installed local value.
Its identity and composition laws retain the complete dependent factory.

~Focus.prototype~ uses the existing ~Prototype.lensGen~ getter/setter/wrapper
constructor with explicit cooked self and inherited method. ~prototype_apply~
agrees with the pure focused update on supplied values. ~prototype_compose~
proves that actual ~DelayedProto.compose~ matches parent-then-child local
wrapping for every supplied self/inherited thunk. These are open construction
laws; they do not assume an equation for unrestricted recursive fixed points.

~modify_frame~ preserves any observation proved insensitive to the setter.
~Independent~ requires both getters to ignore the other setter and requires
the setters to commute. Only with those explicit obligations does
~modify_commute~ commute different focused changes. Changes using a fixed self
fit this law; callbacks that read a changing whole need additional reasoning.

* Keep the admission witness and exact refusal state

~checkedWith~ runs local admission before installing an exposed part, retaining
the proof-bearing admission result as its output. ~checkedWith_ok~ and
~checkedWith_installed~ identify the exact installed value; ~checkedWith_frame~
preserves declared outside observations on success and failure.
~checkedWith_outcome~ retains the admission callback's complete outcome.
~checkedWith_error~ returns the original whole unchanged. ~checked~ is the
same-part specialization.

Composing this step with ~Validation.traverseState~ retains earlier successful
updates, leaves the refusing step's source intact, and skips the suffix. This
is the existing explicit returned-state convention: no successful prefix is
undone. The library does not infer an admission predicate for arbitrary methods.

* Nested pricing methods and continuation

[[file:../../Examples/FocusedPricing.lean][FocusedPricing]] focuses a function-valued pricing method inside a shop's
policy. Context-dependent handling and scaling fragments use cooked self and
inherited prices. The actual delayed chain equals the factory construction for
every context, and the stock and owner remain unchanged. Reversing the two
wrappers changes the endpoint quote from 22 to 19.

Input descriptors admit positive bounded fragment parameters. The output
descriptor checks every quantity from zero through the current stock, returning
a ~Certificate~. ~Certificate.bound~ proves the ceiling for every supported
quantity; ~step_installed~ and ~step_bound~ transfer that proof to the actual
installed pricing method at its returned stock and current context ceiling.
~admit_contract~ binds certificate metadata to the admission context.
The example retains quote 22 after refusing a new
strategy, resumes to 25, and keeps the original quote 8 readable. Frame and
complete batch first-error/final-state laws are kernel checked.

[[file:../../Tests/FunctionalFocusedUpdate.lean][Focused-update controls]] compare 540 actual nested/reversed method compositions
with independent affine coefficients, including outside fields and separate
inventory updates. Another 756 checked batches and 756 continuations compare
outputs and all sampled returned methods with a separate coefficient/intercept
model. Input/output refusals retain the last successful function; two divergent
suffix controls run under the existing bounded supervision contract.

* Budget-indexed allocation with proofs inside the focused part

[[file:../../Examples/FocusedAllocation.lean][FocusedAllocation]] uses ~Allocation budget~ as the focused part of
~Ledger budget~. Moving CPU/IO units conserves the total and carries the budget
proof through the update. ~Factory.focus~ selects a move at the exact budget
index while preserving tenant and revision metadata. A joint pair descriptor
admits runtime allocations; the actual delayed lens installs the proof-bearing
move result. Kernel laws prove conservation, factory feasibility and exact
admitted installation.

960 movement controls use an independent target-share model, with eight
over-budget refusals and eight dependent factory applications. This scenario
demonstrates explicit dependent proofs rather than discovering resource rules
from arbitrary callbacks.

* Source scope

These convenience APIs combine the concrete ~$lens-gen~ formula at source
2084–2154 with the descriptor and parameterized-type constructions at
1359–1433. Failure continuation uses the existing pure returned-state API.
The complete generalized prototype protocol remains the source's stated future
work. No additional evaluator or underlying runtime/compiler proof is involved.
